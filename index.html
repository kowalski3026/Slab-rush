  1. Press and drag on the slab to work each tool. During the pour, switch between the chute and the rake.

  3. Watch the weather. If rain rolls in, hit Tarp it fast.
  4. Thumb test tells you how firm it is. The amber bracket on the meter is the right time for the tool in hand.
