Slab Rush texture detail maps (v152)

asphalt_c.jpg, asphalt_n.jpg
  "Asphalt 031" by ambientCG (https://ambientcg.com/a/Asphalt031), CC0 1.0.
  Resized to 512 px.
slab_n.jpg, slab_m.jpg
  "Concrete 034" by ambientCG (https://ambientcg.com/a/Concrete034), CC0 1.0.
  slab_n is the OpenGL normal map as published; slab_m is a normalised luminance mottle made from the colour map.
*_n.jpg (all others)
  Normal maps generated from the game's own embedded photo textures (high-pass luminance height, Sobel normals).

ambientCG files were copied from github.com/null3d-engine/sample-assets, which mirrors them unchanged under CC0.

Sky lighting (v161)
sky_day.hdr, sky_morning.hdr, sky_golden.hdr, sky_overcast.hdr
  From Poly Haven HDRIs, CC0 1.0: "Rooitou Park", "Spruit Sunrise", "Venice Sunset", "Immenstadter Horn"
  (https://polyhaven.com/hdris). Copied from the three.js and pmndrs/drei-assets repositories, which mirror them unchanged.
  Downsampled to 512x256, sun disc clipped, saturation reduced 40%, sky normalised to mean 1.
